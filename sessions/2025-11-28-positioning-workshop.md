# 🚀 Welcome to Your Sales Page Workshop!

Great! Let's create a sales page that converts visitors into customers. A sales page is different from your homepage - it's laser-focused on ONE offer with ONE goal: getting people to say YES.

## Phase 1: Foundation - Understanding Your Offer

Before we write a single word, I need to understand what we're selling and who we're selling it to.

### Let's start with the basics:

**1. What are you selling on this page?**
   - Is it a product, service, course, membership, or something else?
   - What's it called?

**2. Who is this perfect for?**
   - Be specific! "Small business owners" is too broad
   - Example: "Freelance designers who struggle with client contracts"

**3. What's the ONE transformation or result they get?**
   - Not features - the actual outcome
   - Example: Not "12 modules" but "Land 3 new clients in 60 days"

**4. What's your price point?**
   - This helps me match the right sales page structure
   - High-ticket ($1000+) needs more trust-building than low-ticket

**5. Do you have a name and business name I should use?**
   - Makes our conversation more personal!

Take your time - these answers form the foundation of everything else. Share as much detail as you're comfortable with!
