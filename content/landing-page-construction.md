## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Safety orange (#FF6B35) or construction yellow (#FFC914)
- **Secondary:** Trust blue (#2C5F8D)
- **Accent:** White and light grays
- **Use:** Hard hats, safety vests, and equipment as color cues

### Photography Style
- Bright, natural lighting (avoid dark/gritty construction imagery)
- Mix of men and women
- Diverse team members
- Modern equipment and sites (not dated)
- Professional but approachable

### Stock Photo Keywords (if not using AI)
- "construction team safety training"
- "diverse construction workers"
- "construction manager tablet"
- "construction safety meeting"
- "professional construction crew"

### Trust Elements to Include
- OSHA Authorized badge (if applicable)
- BBB rating
- Industry association memberships
- Secure payment/data icons
- Years in business
